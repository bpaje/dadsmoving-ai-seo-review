Pet Relocation to and from the Philippines

Pet moves are planned around the animal’s route, veterinary records, airline acceptance, destination permits, and quarantine rules—not the household cargo schedule.

Quick answer

Begin pet relocation several months before travel when possible. Lead time varies sharply by destination: Australia and other strict-entry countries may require tests, permits, and quarantine arrangements well before a flight can be booked.

Route planning starts with the animal

Species, breed, age, medical history, crate dimensions, origin, destination, and target travel date determine the permitted route. Airline heat restrictions and breed rules can matter as much as paperwork.

Frequently asked questions

How early should pet relocation begin?

Start as early as possible because veterinary tests, import permits, airline space, and quarantine rules can take longer than the household shipment.

Is airport transport included?

The current service includes residence pickup and air-conditioned transport to the Manila international airport cargo terminal when included in the confirmed route plan.
