Move teams, technology, furniture, and specialist equipment through Metro Manila with a surveyed plan built around the first working day in the new office.

Quick answer

Survey → plan → execute → go-live

  1. Survey. Walk both sites, verify inventory, loading routes, lifts, floor finishes, security controls, and destination readiness.
  2. Plan. Freeze the move matrix, RACI, labels, permit pack, crew waves, IT dependencies, and escalation contacts.
  3. Execute. Install protection, brief crews, load by sequence, track controlled items, and place by floor plan.
  4. Go live. Reconnect authorised equipment, test priority functions, clear packing debris, and log exceptions for close-out.

IT handling with clear authority

Every workstation gets a unique asset-and-destination label. Cable photos stay with that ID; screens and peripherals are packed as matched sets. Server shutdown, backups, privileged access, network reconfiguration, and application acceptance remain with named client IT owners unless a separate technical scope transfers them.

Protection, insurance, and claims clarity

Routes are protected with floor covering, corner guards, door-jamb padding, lift wraps, and controlled trolley paths. The written proposal should state declared value, coverage limits, exclusions, custody boundaries, and the claims process before mobilisation—“insured” is not a substitute for those details.

Coverage across Makati, BGC, Ortigas, QC, and Alabang

Makati and BGC towers often enforce service-lift and after-hours windows. Ortigas staging depends on dense mixed-use traffic and limited curb space. Quezon City sites may combine campuses, government security, and longer north–south routes. Alabang moves often involve business parks and gated compounds close to Dad’s Muntinlupa base.

Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should a Manila office move be planned?

Six to eight weeks is a practical planning window for a medium office. Buildings with strict permit, loading-bay, or weekend requirements may need earlier booking.

Can Dad’s move servers and workstations?

Yes, with a tagged and sequenced plan. Client IT owners still approve shutdown, backups, network changes, privileged access, and application acceptance.

How is an office-move estimate built?

The written estimate uses surveyed inventory, crew and vehicle hours, packing materials, specialist handling, access constraints, storage, permits, and after-hours requirements.
