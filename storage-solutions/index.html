Quick answer

Storage pricing depends on occupied volume, handling, protection requirements, and duration. Dad’s can collect and pack items, then place them into regular warehouse, climate-controlled, private-room, records, or commercial storage after confirming suitability.

Choose storage by item, not by label

Household furniture, art, records, electronics, machinery, and inventory do not share the same handling needs. The survey identifies moisture sensitivity, retrieval frequency, declared value, and access requirements before the storage method is selected.

Facility protections described on the current site

The facility description includes 24/7 CCTV, roving guards, a night curfew, two dedicated hydrant-and-hose systems, and staff trained by the Muntinlupa Fire Brigade. Insurance and any climate-control requirement should be confirmed in writing for the specific inventory.

Business use cases

Records retrieval, office-decommission staging, IT equipment, promotional materials, e-commerce stock, construction materials, automotive parts, and white goods can be scoped without forcing everything into palletised storage.
