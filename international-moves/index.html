One route plan, not a generic five-step script

The current journey begins with the shipment itself: what is moving, the destination, visa position, time constraints, and items that may trigger inspection. Dad’s then coordinates origin packing and export formalities with a vetted destination partner for clearance and final delivery.

What door-to-door covers

  • Volume survey and route-specific quotation
  • Export-standard packing and itemised inventory
  • Philippine export and destination import coordination
  • Sea or air freight, final delivery, unpacking, and debris removal as scoped

IAM network and 196-country reach

Dad’s is a member of the International Association of Movers. That network supports handoffs to professional destination agents while keeping one accountable origin contact for the client.

Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should I book?

Allow 6–8 weeks for a survey, export packing, customs documents, and destination-specific checks. Strict biosecurity destinations may need longer.

Do you manage customs paperwork?

Dad’s coordinates Philippine export formalities and destination import requirements with its international partner network. Final requirements depend on route and visa status.

Can I ship less than a container?

Yes. LCL shipping prices shared container space by volume and is often suitable for smaller households.
