Moving from Manila to Australia: Cost, Customs & Timeline

A door-to-door guide for households and organisations relocating from the Philippines to Australia—what it may cost, what biosecurity requires, and how long to budget.

Quick answer

A full household move from Manila to Australia may cost ₱180,000–₱950,000 illustrative sample, depending on shipment size and destination, and may take 6–10 weeks door-to-door after packing. All figures require operations-team confirmation before publication or quoting.

Australia applies strict biosecurity controls: declare and clean outdoor, gardening, sporting, timber, plant, and soil-exposed items before shipment.

What Australian customs and biosecurity require

Australia’s Department of Agriculture, Fisheries and Forestry inspects incoming household goods. Wooden furniture with soil or pest risk may need treatment; bicycles, golf clubs, camping and gardening equipment must be clean; food, plant material, and untreated timber may be restricted; and the packing list must be specific.

Visa and household-goods status

Eligibility for duty treatment depends on immigration status, ownership, use, and timing. The earlier prototype’s broad duty-free statement must be verified against the client’s visa subclass and current Australian Border Force and biosecurity guidance before production.

Typical door-to-door sequence

  1. Virtual or in-home survey, route check, and written quote.
  2. Export packing, itemised inventory, and Philippine documents.
  3. Sea or air freight, Australian customs, and biosecurity inspection.
  4. Destination delivery, unpacking, and debris removal as scoped.

Frequently asked questions

Can I ship my car from Manila to Australia?

Yes, but vehicles follow separate import approval and Australian Design Rules compliance. Quote the vehicle separately from household goods.

What can't I bring into Australia?

Firearms and some plant, animal, food, timber, and traditional-medicine products are restricted or prohibited. Confirm the current list before packing.

How long does sea freight take?

The reviewed planning range is 6–10 weeks door-to-door after packing, but sailing schedules, destination city, biosecurity inspection, and customs can change it.
