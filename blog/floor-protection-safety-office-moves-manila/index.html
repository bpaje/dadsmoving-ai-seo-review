The controls below are intended for a surveyed, building-approved move plan; project owners should confirm any route, permit, technical, or insurance requirement before execution.

Survey the travel path

Trace vehicle-to-workpoint routes at both sites. Note marble, timber, carpet tiles, ramps, fire doors, low ceilings, glass corners, lift thresholds, public crossings, and any surface with a building-specific protection rule.

Install before staging

Protection goes down before crates, trolleys, or dismantled furniture enter the route. Tape and joins must not create trip hazards; emergency exits, fire equipment, and accessibility routes remain clear.

Brief the real hazards

The toolbox talk names lifting limits, team-lift items, trolley rules, pinch points, restricted rooms, housekeeping, hydration, shift limits, incident reporting, and stop-work authority. Generic “be careful” briefings do not control a route.

Inspect between waves

Check displaced boards, wet areas, torn lift wraps, blocked exits, and accumulated packaging after each load wave. Photograph pre-existing damage and final condition with the building representative.

Does protection guarantee zero damage?

No method removes all risk. Protection, controlled routes, supervision, documentation, and clear claims handling reduce and manage it.
