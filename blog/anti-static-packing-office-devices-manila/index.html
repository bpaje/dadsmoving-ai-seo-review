Choose anti-static bags, foam, crates, humidity, orientation, and handling controls for high-value office devices. The controls below are intended for a surveyed, building-approved move plan; project owners should confirm any route, permit, technical, or insurance requirement before execution.

Match the protection

Follow manufacturer guidance. Anti-static bags control electrostatic discharge but do not replace impact cushioning, moisture protection, or a fitted crate.

Prepare safely

Authorised technicians power down, remove batteries or consumables where required, secure moving parts, cap ports, and record condition.

Control transport

Confirm heat, humidity, vibration, tilt, stacking, and vehicle limits. Use reusable fitted crates and shock indicators when justified.
