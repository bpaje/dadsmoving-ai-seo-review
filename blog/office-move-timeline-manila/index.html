A 6–8 week Manila office move calendar covering surveys, permits, labels, IT, safety, night loadout, and Day-1 checks. The controls below are intended for a surveyed, building-approved move plan; project owners should confirm any route, permit, technical, or insurance requirement before execution.

Week 8–6: pre-move survey and inventory

Walk origin and destination with facilities and IT. Count by department and asset class, photograph access routes, measure service lifts and doors, and log fragile, high-value, regulated, or unusually heavy items. The signed inventory becomes the quote and variance baseline.

Week 6–4: permits and elevator scheduling

Request contractor accreditation, COI wording, worker and vehicle lists, loading-bay reservations, lift capacity, after-hours HVAC, deposits, and floor-protection rules from both buildings. In Makati, a Friday move can fail simply because one administration closed permit changes on Wednesday.

Week 4–3: labels and packing cadence

Freeze the destination floor plan and issue floor-zone-workpoint codes. Pack archives and low-use storage first; leave active teams, pantry essentials, and incident-response equipment until their wave. Print labels on two sides and on the crate lid.

Week 3–2: IT and AV plan

Assign shutdown and acceptance owners, photograph cable layouts, export device lists, verify backups, reserve anti-static packaging, and define the server and network cutover. Test the destination WAN before equipment leaves origin.

Week 2–1: protection and safety brief

Confirm trolley routes, install dates for floor and lift protection, crew call sheets, emergency exits, first aid, toolbox talk, and stop-work contacts. Reconfirm destination power, cooling, lighting, security, and access cards.

Move week and Day 1

Stage by department, load in reverse delivery order, keep controlled items logged, and use night hours only within approved shift limits. Before handover, test network, priority apps, printers, phones, conferencing, access control, and room readiness.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best time for a Makati office move?

Often Friday evening through the weekend, provided both buildings approve loading, service lifts, security, HVAC, and noise. Avoid assuming “after hours” means unrestricted access.

How long does the physical move take?

A small office may move in one night; a medium office often needs a weekend; complex sites can run in waves. Surveyed volume and access determine the answer.

Are after-hours fees common?

Yes. Buildings, security, lift operators, HVAC, parking, and crews may all carry after-hours charges, which should be listed separately.
