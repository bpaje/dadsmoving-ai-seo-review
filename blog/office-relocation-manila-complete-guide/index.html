The Complete Manila Office Relocation Guide (Planning to Go-Live)

A practical control document for the weeks between “we are moving” and a stable first working day.

Quick answer

Start 6–8 weeks before the target go-live. Lock the two-building access plan, inventory, decision owners, IT shutdown and acceptance sequence, labels, protection, and budget before booking crews.

Timeline and checklist

Count backwards from Day 1. Weeks 8–6 establish inventory and constraints; 6–4 secure permits and lift windows; 4–3 freeze labels and packing; 3–2 rehearse IT dependencies; 2–1 brief safety and user communications. Move week is execution, not discovery.

Stakeholders and roles

Name one sponsor, one move captain, department coordinators, facilities leads at both buildings, security, finance, HR/comms, vendor owners, and an IT change authority. A RACI prevents a mover from being asked to approve a server shutdown or a building admin from deciding desk placement.

Building coordination and COI

Ask both administrations for permit lead times, contractor accreditation, Certificate of Insurance wording, loading-bay dimensions, service-lift capacity, allowed hours, worker lists, vehicle lists, floor protection, deposits, and restoration rules.

IT and data-safe moves

Back up, label, photograph, disconnect, pack, seal, transport, reconnect, test, and sign off in that order. Chain-of-custody logs belong on devices or media whose loss would create a security incident.

Packing and labeling system

Use a destination code that survives organisation changes: floor-zone-workpoint or room-rack-position. Colour helps crews, but the printed code remains authoritative for colour-blind readers and photocopied lists.

Floor protection and safety

Map trolley routes and pinch points. Install protection before stockpiling crates, inspect it between waves, and document pre-existing damage. Toolbox talks cover lifting, routes, restricted rooms, emergency exits, and stop-work authority.

After-hours and weekend moves

A Friday-night loadout works only if permits, access cards, lights, air-conditioning, elevators, security, and destination acceptance are active after hours. Budget the building fees and realistic crew shift limits.

Budget and pricing models

Compare fixed-fee and time-and-materials quotes on identical assumptions. Separate variable access risk, client-caused waiting time, change requests, storage, and reinstatement rather than burying them in a headline figure.

Day-1 go-live

Test WAN and Wi-Fi, identity access, priority applications, printers, video conferencing, room booking, phones, access control, pantry and washrooms. Publish a single issue channel and triage blockers before cosmetic snags.

Post-move support

Keep a small crew for crate recovery, furniture adjustments, missing-item tracing, and exceptions. Close only after inventory variance, damage observations, disposal records, and access returns are reconciled.

Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should a Manila office move be planned?

Six to eight weeks is a practical planning window for a medium office. Buildings with strict permit, loading-bay, or weekend requirements may need earlier booking.

Can Dad’s move servers and workstations?

Yes, with a tagged and sequenced plan. Client IT owners still approve shutdown, backups, network changes, privileged access, and application acceptance.

How is an office-move estimate built?

The written estimate uses surveyed inventory, crew and vehicle hours, packing materials, specialist handling, access constraints, storage, permits, and after-hours requirements.
