How far ahead should a Manila office move be planned?

Six to eight weeks is a practical planning window for a medium office. Buildings with strict permit, loading-bay, or weekend requirements may need earlier booking.

Can Dad’s move servers and workstations?

Yes, with a tagged and sequenced plan. Client IT owners still approve shutdown, backups, network changes, privileged access, and application acceptance.
