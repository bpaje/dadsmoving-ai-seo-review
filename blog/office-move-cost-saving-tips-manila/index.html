The controls below are intended for a surveyed, building-approved move plan; project owners should confirm any route, permit, technical, or insurance requirement before execution.

Move less

Approve reuse, sale, donation, recycling, storage, and disposal before packing. Every unnecessary desk adds handling and vehicle space.

Freeze decisions

Late floor-plan and roster changes cause relabelling and waiting. Route exceptions through one move captain.

Save through logistics

Consolidate permits, reserve uninterrupted lift blocks, use reusable crates, and stage low-use items early. Do not cut survey, protection, backups, or supervision.
