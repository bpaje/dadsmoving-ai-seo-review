Protect data in transit with classification, sealed custody, authorised handlers, access logs, incident response, and reconciliation. The controls below are intended for a surveyed, building-approved move plan; project owners should confirm any route, permit, technical, or insurance requirement before execution.

Classify and minimise

Separate ordinary equipment from devices, media, and records whose loss would cause harm. Securely dispose of authorised obsolete material before moving.

Record custody

For controlled items log ID, seal, time, releasing and receiving people, vehicle, and exceptions. Labels reveal destination and handling—not confidential content.

Prepare for incidents

Define escalation for a broken seal, missing item, route delay, or unauthorised access. Reconcile before releasing the controlled crew.
