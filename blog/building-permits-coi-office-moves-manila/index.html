The controls below are intended for a surveyed, building-approved move plan; project owners should confirm any route, permit, technical, or insurance requirement before execution.

Ask for the building move pack

Request rules from origin and destination rather than reusing an old checklist. Premium towers may require contractor accreditation, IDs, vehicle plates, method statements, safety plans, approved materials, deposits, and several business days of review.

Book loading and lifts as one system

A loading bay without a service lift is not a slot. Confirm bay dimensions, truck height, dock leveller, lift size and load, protective wrapping, operator availability, route to the suite, queueing rules, and contingency time.

Give the insurer exact COI fields

Sample fields to confirm are insured legal name, certificate holder, covered operations, site addresses, dates, limits, and additional-insured wording if required. Never invent limits in a permit form; use the issued certificate and building template.

Close the permit loop

Keep written approval, payment receipts, endorsed worker and vehicle lists, building contacts, and the approved time window in the move-day pack. Reconfirm 48 hours before mobilisation because tenant works and lift outages can change availability.

Frequently asked questions

How long do building permits take?

Allow at least several business days; some buildings need one to two weeks when accreditation, COI review, or deposits are involved. Ask the administration for its current lead time.

What if the COI wording is rejected?

Return the building’s exact certificate-holder and coverage wording to the insurer or broker. Do not edit an issued certificate yourself.
