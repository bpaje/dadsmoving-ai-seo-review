Reduce damage with condition records, fitted padding, corner guards, lift wraps, floor systems, controlled routes, and inspections. The controls below are intended for a surveyed, building-approved move plan; project owners should confirm any route, permit, technical, or insurance requirement before execution.

Record condition

Photograph items, lifts, walls, floors, and pinch points. Decide when dismantling is safer than forcing a turn.

Fit protection

Use clean pads, guards, secure moving parts, suitable floor boards or film, and building-approved lift wraps without trip hazards.

Supervise handoffs

Use spotters, prevent mixed fragile loads, inspect between waves, and log any damage immediately.
