Run a Manila office pre-move survey covering inventory, measurements, photos, building access, IT, risks, and the destination floor plan. The controls below are intended for a surveyed, building-approved move plan; project owners should confirm any route, permit, technical, or insurance requirement before execution.

Set the survey scope

Survey both sites with facilities, IT, security, and the move lead. Record every room, open-office zone, storage area, server or comms room, pantry, reception, archive, and restricted space; “typical floor” assumptions hide the assets that cause change orders.

Build a usable inventory

Count desks, chairs, pedestals, screens, crates, shelving bays, appliances, artwork, safes, racks, and specialist devices. Add dimensions and estimated weight where lift, doorway, manual-handling, or crating decisions depend on them.

Photograph evidence, not decoration

Capture labels, serials where authorised, cable layouts, existing damage, narrow turns, ramps, loading bays, lift interiors, overhead clearance, floor finishes, and parking approach. Name files by room and item so they remain usable during execution.

Measure and reconcile

Measure the tightest door, lift, corridor turn, dock height, and destination footprint. Reconcile the survey count to the floor plan and department owner; mark dispose, donate, store, direct-move, and specialist-move decisions before the estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Who should attend the survey?

The move captain, facilities, IT, security or building liaison, and the mover’s surveyor. Department coordinators can verify specialist or shared areas.

Should every monitor be serialised?

Only when asset policy requires it. A unique move ID tied to an authorised asset export is usually more practical than copying serial numbers by hand.
