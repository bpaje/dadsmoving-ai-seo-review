Local Movers in Manila — Careful, Surveyed Moves

A local move is planned around the actual inventory, access rules, crew hours, and Metro Manila travel window—not a generic truck booking.

Quick answer

Most Metro Manila condo and small-house moves can be packed and delivered within one planned move day, while larger homes may need a separate packing day. Final duration and cost depend on surveyed volume, stairs or elevators, travel distance, and building time windows.

A local move built from the survey

Dad’s starts by recording rooms, fragile pieces, access points, parking, elevator windows, and destination placement. That inventory becomes the basis for crew size, vehicle allocation, packing materials, and the written estimate.

What the service can include

  • Professional packing with room and destination labels
  • Condo gate passes, loading-bay and elevator coordination
  • Padded transport, delivery, unpacking, and debris removal
  • Separate handling for artwork, IT equipment, and oversize furniture

Metro Manila timing

Traffic is treated as a schedule input. A Makati-to-BGC move may be geographically short but still depends on loading-bay windows and EDSA or C5 conditions; the move plan works backwards from the destination slot.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a local move in Metro Manila take?

Most condo or small-house moves finish in one day after a survey confirms volume, building access, and travel time. Larger homes may need a separate packing day.

Do you coordinate elevator bookings?

Yes. The move plan records each building’s loading bay, service-elevator window, gate-pass requirements, and contact person before move day.

Is packing included?

Professional packing can be included in the written scope, along with delivery, unpacking, and debris removal.
