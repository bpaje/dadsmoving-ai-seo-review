Frequently asked questions

Can the move happen over a weekend?

Yes. Weekend or after-hours sequencing is commonly used to protect business hours, subject to building permits, elevator windows, and crew premiums in the written estimate.

Do you disconnect and reconnect IT?

The scope can include tagged workstation moves and coordinated reconnection. Server shutdown, network changes, and application validation remain with the client’s authorised IT owners unless separately agreed.

Does the move include insurance?

Coverage and declared-value limits are confirmed in the written proposal. Ask for the policy summary, exclusions, and claims process before approval.
