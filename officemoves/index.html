Frequently Asked Questions

What makes you the best office movers in Manila?

At Dad’s Moving & Storage, we combine affordable rates with professional, reliable services tailored for corporate clients. With years of experience moving offices and business facilities in and around Manila, we know how to minimize downtime and maximize efficiency. Our team is trained, punctual, and equipped to handle everything from IT equipment, furniture, data centers, specialist equipment to sensitive documents—making your business relocation smooth and stress-free.

How much does a move cost?

The cost of a corporate move depends on several factors, including the size of your office, the volume of items, distance, and any special services required (like packing & storage). We offer transparent pricing and free onsite assessments, so you know exactly what to expect—no hidden fees, just honest rates. Get in touch so we can schedule a free survey for an accurate quote.

How long does a move take?

Most corporate moves in Metro Manila can be completed in one to five days. However, the exact timeline depends on the scope & scale of the move, access to both locations, and preparation. We work with you to create a moving schedule that minimizes disruption to your business operations.

What to look for in a mover?

When choosing a moving company, look for experience, clear communication, proper licensing, and positive customer reviews. For corporate moves, it’s also critical that your movers understand proper logistics, timing, and how to handle specialized equipment. Dad’s Moving & Storage checks all those boxes—plus we offer some of the most competitive rates in Manila.

What do I need to prepare for in a move?

Good preparation is key to a successful move. Here’s what you should do:

  • Assign a move coordinator from your team
  • Schedule your move well in advance
  • Inform your employees and clients
  • Label and organize your office equipment and files
  • Back up all digital data
  • Pick a a highly rated moving partner
Don’t worry—we’ll provide a checklist and guide you through each step.
How far in advance should I book a move?

For corporate moves, we recommend booking at least 6 to 8 weeks in advance to ensure availability and allow time for proper planning. If your move involves multiple departments, large inventories, or special logistics (like IT disconnections or phased relocations), 4 to 6 weeks is ideal. Early booking helps us align the schedule, assign the right crew, and coordinate any additional services you may need—like packing, storage, or weekend moves.

What's included in a move?

Our standard corporate moving package includes:

  • Dedicated Office Move Specialist 
  • Project Move Management 
  • Disassembly and reassembly of furniture
  • Wrapping and protection of office equipment
  • Safe transport with our fleet of vehicles
  • Unloading into new office space 
Additional services like packing, unpacking, storage, reinstatement or disposal can be added based on your needs.
 
Do you offer temporary storage if the new office isn’t move-in ready?

Yes, we do. Dad’s Moving & Storage offers secure, short- and long-term, regular & climate controlled storage solutions for corporate clients. If your new office isn’t ready yet, we can safely store your office furniture, equipment, and documents in our monitored, weather-protected facility until your new space is ready for occupancy. We’ll coordinate the timing of the delivery based on your schedule.

Can you handle specialized items and IT equipment (Data centers & scientific equipment)

Yes, we can. Dad’s Moving & Storage has extensive experience handling sensitive and high-value office equipment, including servers, computer monitors, data centers, networking gear, and other IT assets. Our team uses secure, static free packaging, and proper handling techniques & equipment to ensure your items is protected during transit. If needed, we can also coordinate with your IT department for safe disconnection, labeling, and setup at your new location.

Can you perform moves outside regular business hours or on weekends?

Absolutely. We understand that minimizing disruption is critical for any business, so we offer flexible scheduling—including after-hours, weekends, and even holidays when needed. Our team can work around your business operations to ensure a smooth, efficient move without interfering with your workday.

Can you explain the moving process?

Sure! Here’s how a typical corporate move with us works:
1. Initial Consultation & Site Survey – We assess your needs and provide a free & bespoke quote.
2. Planning & Coordination – We create a moving strategy that fits your timeline and business schedule.
3. Our team dismantles furniture, racks and workstation equipment

5. Moving Day – We transport everything safely and efficiently.
